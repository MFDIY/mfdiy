How it works

01

Run the bridge

A small app on your gaming PC reads the game's local telemetry and shares it on your home network.

02

Build your layout

Drag widgets onto screens and bind them to data and in-game actions. No code.

03

Open it anywhere

Any browser becomes your MFD: phone, tablet, or a dedicated touchscreen.
